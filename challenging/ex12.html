Challenging · Exercise 12

Form styling

Explicit labels make fields understandable to assistive technology and easier to activate. Native required and email validation work alongside clear keyboard focus styles.

Live result

All fields have visible labels and are keyboard accessible

Solution markup

<form class="ex12-form" action="#" method="get">
  <div>
    <label for="email">Email address</label>
    <input id="email" name="email" type="email" required>
  </div>
  <button type="submit">Send message</button>
</form>
